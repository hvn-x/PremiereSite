    What an alert looks like

    Every alert is a card with the cover art, the date in your timezone, and a countdown to what's next. A button underneath opens the title or shows where to watch it.

    What it follows

    Search for anything with /track and pick it from the list. Alerts go to your DMs, or to a server channel with an optional ping.

    • Anime

      Every new episode, at the minute it airs.

      From AniList. Import your AniList or MyAnimeList list.

    • Movies

      In theaters, digital release, and the day it reaches a streaming service.

      From TMDB.

    • TV

      New episodes and season premieres. A whole-season drop is one alert.

      From TMDB. Import a Trakt watchlist.

    • Games

      Launch day, new platforms, and release-date changes.

      From IGDB.

    • News

      Steam game news, YouTube uploads, subreddits, or any site with a feed.

      Type the name. Premiere finds the feed.

    Commands

    Server commands need the Manage Server permission. Everything else works for anyone, including in DMs.

    • /trackFollow a title or feed
    • /listEverything you follow
    • /untrackStop following something
    • /syncImport AniList, MAL or Trakt
    • /timezoneSet your local time
    • /settingsReminders and quiet hours
    • /server setupWhere server alerts go
    • /delete-my-dataErase everything about you
