What an alert looks like

Every alert is a card with the cover art, the date in your timezone, and a countdown to what's next. A button underneath opens the title or shows where to watch it.

What it follows

Search for anything with /track and pick it from the list. Alerts go to your DMs, or to a server channel with an optional ping.

Anime
Every new episode, at the minute it airs. From AniList. You can import what you're watching from AniList or MyAnimeList.
Movies
In theaters, digital release, and the day it lands on a streaming service. From TMDB.
TV
New episodes and new season premieres. Whole-season drops arrive as one alert. From TMDB. You can import a Trakt watchlist.
Games
Launch day, ports to new platforms, and release-date changes. From IGDB.
News
A Steam game's news and patch notes, a YouTube channel's uploads, a subreddit, or any website with a feed. Type the name and Premiere finds the feed.

Commands

Server commands need the Manage Server permission. Everything else works for anyone, including in DMs.

  • /trackFollow an anime, movie, show, game or feed
  • /listEverything you follow, with countdowns
  • /untrackStop following something
  • /syncImport from AniList, MyAnimeList or Trakt
  • /timezoneSet your timezone for release times
  • /settingsReminders, quiet hours, and which alerts you get
  • /server setupChoose where server alerts go
  • /delete-my-dataErase everything stored about you
