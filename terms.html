What Premiere does

Premiere sends alerts about releases of anime, movies, TV shows and games, and new posts on RSS feeds you choose. It's free and provided as a hobby project.

No guarantees

Release information comes from AniList, TMDB, IGDB, MyAnimeList, Trakt and the feeds you add. It can be wrong, late or change without notice, and an alert may arrive late or not at all. The bot may be unavailable at times, and features may change or be removed. Using it fairly

  • Follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines when using Premiere.
  • Don't use Premiere to spam people. Server admins using role-DM mode should only pick roles whose members want the alerts.
  • Don't add RSS feeds you aren't allowed to read, or try to make the bot fetch private or internal addresses.
  • Don't try to overload, break or misuse the bot or the services it relies on.

Access may be limited or blocked for anyone who breaks these rules.
